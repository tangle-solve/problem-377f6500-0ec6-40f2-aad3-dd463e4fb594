### CHEM-000514: Operational electric-field distributions at reactive aqueous surfaces
- **Subfield:** Liquid-state and interfacial physical chemistry
- **Open:** Determine local electric-field distributions and lifetimes experienced by reacting interfacial solutes, with consistent mappings between spectroscopy and simulations. Test their quantitative contribution to rate changes.
- **Status:** A 2025 study found no exceptionally strong or long-lived fields at pristine neutral air–water interfaces. Charged and compositionally complex reactive interfaces require separate measurements.
- **Type:** experimental | theory
- **Why:** It would replace qualitative field explanations with constrained reaction models.
- **Key works:** J. C. Shirley et al. (2025), *Reevaluating Anomalous Electric Fields at the Air–Water Interface: A Surface-Specific Spectroscopic Survey*, [DOI:10.1021/jacs.5c14541](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c14541); [arXiv:2508.15422](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15422).
